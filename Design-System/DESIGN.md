# Design System Inspired by Claude (Anthropic)

> Category: Brands

> Surface: web

*Warm terracotta accent, clean editorial layout — a literary salon reimagined as a product page.*

Claude's interface is built on a parchment-toned canvas with warm, yellow-brown neutrals and organic illustrations. Terracotta brand accent, serif headlines, sans UI, and a ring-shadow depth system that avoids traditional drop shadows. Every detail evokes high-quality paper, not a digital surface.

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| background | Parchment | `#f5f4ed` | Page canvas — warm cream evoking premium paper |
| foreground | Near Black | `#141413` | Body text, headings, dark-theme background |
| accent | Terracotta Brand | `#c96442` | Primary CTAs, highest-signal brand moments |
| surface | Dark Surface | `#30302e` | Cards, panels, dark-theme elevated surfaces |
| muted | Olive Gray | `#5e5d59` | Secondary text, metadata, captions |
| border | Border Cream | `#f0eee6` | Rules, dividers, standard card borders |

## Typography
- **Display:** Georgia — weights 400, 700 — fallbacks: 'Times New Roman', serif
- **Body:** system-ui — weights 400, 500, 600 — fallbacks: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif
- **Mono:** 'SF Mono' — weights 400 — fallbacks: 'SFMono-Regular', Consolas, 'Liberation Mono', Menlo, Courier, monospace

## Voice & Tone

- **Adjectives:** warm, unhurried, intellectual, editorial, trustworthy, human
- **Tone:** Thoughtful and quiet. Like a magazine editor who also happens to build AI — every word is chosen with care, every surface feels considered. Never shouts.

### Messaging pillars
- Thoughtful companion — the interface invites conversation, not command
- Warmth as differentiation — parchment tones and terracotta say 'human' in a cold AI landscape
- Editorial quality — pages paced like magazine features with serif-driven hierarchy

### Vocabulary
- **Use:** thoughtful, carefully, designed, considered, warm, human, quiet, editorial, companion, trustworthy
- **Avoid:** revolutionary, disruptive, powerful, cutting-edge, unleash, supercharge, master, dominate, one-click

## Imagery

- **Style:** Organic, hand-drawn-feeling vector illustrations in terracotta, black, and muted green. Abstract and conceptual — not literal product diagrams.
- **Subjects:** Conceptual AI interaction illustrations, Product screenshots of chat interface, Dark UI screenshots for contrast against warm canvas
- **Treatment:** Generous border-radius (16–32px) on media. Embedded media with rounded corners. No heavy drop shadows.
- **Avoid:** 3D renders, Geometric tech icons, Generic stock photography, Purple gradients, Emoji as hero visuals

## Layout

- **Radius:** Standard: 8px. Scale: 4px → 6px → 8px → 12px → 16px → 24px → 32px.
- **Border weight:** 1px
- **Spacing:** 8px baseline grid. Scale: 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 24, 30, 32, 48, 64, 80, 120px.

### Posture rules
- Alternate light/dark sections — Parchment → Near Black → Parchment, like chapters in a book
- Ring-shadow depth system: 0px 0px 0px 1px warm rings instead of drop shadows
- Asymmetric button padding: secondary 0px 12px 0px 8px (icon-first), primary 8px 16px
- Input focus uses Focus Blue (#3898ec) — the only cool color, for accessibility only
- Generous border-radius: buttons 8–12px, cards 8px, featured 16–32px
- No cool neutrals — every border, fill, and shadow carries a yellow-brown undertone
- Serif headings at weight 500 only — no bold, consistent editorial voice
